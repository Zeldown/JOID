# Commercial Grants

Public record of commercial licenses granted for JOID under Section 6 of the [license](LICENSE.md).

Each entry documents a specific grant issued by the copyright holder. A project that is not listed here has **not** been granted a commercial license and must either request one (see [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md)) or stay within the non-commercial boundary defined in Section 3 of the [license](LICENSE.md).

## How to be listed

Follow the procedure in [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md). Once a grant is issued, this file is updated with the entry and the grantee is notified.

## Revocations

Grants may be revoked under Section 9 of the [license](LICENSE.md) (Termination). Revoked entries are kept in this file with a strikethrough and a dated note, so the public record stays auditable.
