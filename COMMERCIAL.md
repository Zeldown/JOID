# Commercial License

Commercial Use of JOID — as defined in Section 3 of the [license](LICENSE.md) — requires a prior written commercial license from the copyright holder.

## How to request one

Send a direct message on Discord to **`zeldown`** with the following:

1. A short description of the intended use (what the UI does, where it is shown, who the end users are).
2. The approximate scale (number of users, number of transactions, expected revenue range).
3. The URL of the project, if public.

You will typically receive a reply within **24 to 48 hours**.

## Approved commercial licenses

The public list of granted commercial licenses is maintained in [COMMERCIAL_GRANTS.md](COMMERCIAL_GRANTS.md).

## Non-commercial use

If your use falls under Section 3's definition of non-commercial — general gameplay UIs (HUD, settings, scoreboard, inventory, chat, minimap, launcher, login screen), servers that accept donations without a JOID-built shop, internal tools, open-source projects — you do **not** need a commercial license. Just comply with the attribution and share-alike rules in the [license](LICENSE.md).
