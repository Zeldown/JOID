---
summary: The side that the fill of a progress bar grows from.
---
Learn: [Controls](/use/learn/interactivity/controls/#show-a-progress).

## LEFT_TO_RIGHT

The fill grows from the left edge.

## RIGHT_TO_LEFT

The fill grows from the right edge.

## TOP_TO_BOTTOM

The fill grows from the top edge.

## BOTTOM_TO_TOP

The fill grows from the bottom edge.

## isHorizontal()

If `true`, the fill grows along the width.
