---
summary: Easing curves along a power of 3.
---

Each constant is also in [[TweenEquations]], as `CUBIC_IN`, `CUBIC_OUT` and `CUBIC_IN_OUT`.

Learn: [Animation](/use/learn/style-and-motion/animation/).

## CubicTweenEquation()

Creates a curve of this family, for a subclass.

## IN

Starts slowly, with a power of 3.

## OUT

Ends slowly, with a power of 3.

## IN_OUT

Starts and ends slowly, with a power of 3.
