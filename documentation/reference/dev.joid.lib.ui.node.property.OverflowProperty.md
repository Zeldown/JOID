---
summary: What a node does with the children that go past its bounds.
---
Learn: [Layout](/use/learn/describing-the-ui/layout/#clip-what-goes-beyond-a-node).

## NONE

The children are drawn and receive the input past the bounds.

## HIDDEN

The children are clipped to the bounds of the node.

## SCROLL

The children are clipped to the bounds, and the wheel or a scrollbar scrolls them.
