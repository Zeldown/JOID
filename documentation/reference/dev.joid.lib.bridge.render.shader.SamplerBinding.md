Learn: [Textures and samplers](/build/pipeline/#textures-and-samplers).

## of(ITexture,TextureSampling)

Pairs the texture with its sampling.

## getTexture()

Returns the texture to bind.

## getSampling()

Returns the filter, the wrap and the mipmap flag to sample with.
