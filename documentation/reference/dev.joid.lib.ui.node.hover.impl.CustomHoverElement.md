---
summary: Places a tooltip element of your own at the mouse, on the node or at a fixed point.
---
Learn: [Hover and tooltips](/use/learn/interactivity/hover-and-tooltips/#show-a-tooltip).

## follow(IHoverElement)

Draws the element at the mouse.

## fixed(IHoverElement)

Draws the element from the top-left corner of the canvas.

## relative(IHoverElement)

Draws the element from the top-left corner of the hovered node.

## CustomHoverElement(IHoverElement,HoverElementPosition)

Called by a subclass with the element and its position.
