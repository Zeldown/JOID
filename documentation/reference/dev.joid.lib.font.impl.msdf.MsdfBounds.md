---
summary: A rectangle of an MSDF glyph, either on 1 of the em with Y up or in pixels of the atlas.
---

[[MsdfGlyph]] holds two: its plane bounds around the origin of the glyph, and its atlas bounds in the image.

Learn: [MSDF text](/use/in-depth/msdf-text/).

## of(float,float,float,float)

Creates the rectangle from its left, bottom, right and top edges.

## getLeft()

Returns the left edge.

## getBottom()

Returns the bottom edge.

## getRight()

Returns the right edge.

## getTop()

Returns the top edge.
