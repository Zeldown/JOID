summary: What the rounded and circle shaders mask: the texture tinted by the color, the texture alone, or the color alone.
---

Learn: [Shaders](/use/learn/going-further/shaders/).

## AUTO

Masks the bound texture multiplied by the vertex color, the case of shapes and images.

## TEXTURE

Masks the bound texture alone, premultiplied, the case of a shader pass on a framebuffer.

## COLOR

Masks the vertex color alone and ignores the texture.
