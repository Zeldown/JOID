---
summary: Easing curves along a power of 2.
---

Each constant is also in [[TweenEquations]], as `QUAD_IN`, `QUAD_OUT` and `QUAD_IN_OUT`.

Learn: [Animation](/use/learn/style-and-motion/animation/).

## QuadTweenEquation()

Creates a curve of this family, for a subclass.

## IN

Starts slowly, with a power of 2.

## OUT

Ends slowly, with a power of 2.

## IN_OUT

Starts and ends slowly, with a power of 2.
