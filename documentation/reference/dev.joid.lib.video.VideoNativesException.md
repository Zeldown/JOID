---
summary: The FFmpeg natives that videos need cannot be installed on this machine.
---

A video whose natives fail to install fails with this exception as its error; the message names the cause and the fix.

Learn: [Images and media](/use/learn/describing-the-ui/media/).

## VideoNativesException(String)

Creates the exception with its message.

## VideoNativesException(String,Throwable)

Creates the exception with its message and its cause.
