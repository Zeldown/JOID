---
summary: Transition that grows a UI from 75 % of its size when it opens and shrinks it back when it closes, in 130 ms, around its anchor.
---
It is the transition of a popup.

Learn: [Transitions](/use/learn/style-and-motion/transitions/).

## create()

Creates a pop transition with both of its steps enabled.

## PopTransition()

Called by a subclass that changes the pop transition.
