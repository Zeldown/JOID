---
summary: Easing curves that bounce, like a ball that falls and rebounds.
---

Each constant is also in [[TweenEquations]], as `BOUNCE_IN`, `BOUNCE_OUT` and `BOUNCE_IN_OUT`.

Learn: [Animation](/use/learn/style-and-motion/animation/).

## BounceTweenEquation()

Creates a curve of this family, for a subclass.

## IN

Bounces at the start.

## OUT

Bounces at the end, like a ball that falls.

## IN_OUT

Bounces at the start and at the end.
