---
summary: Easing curves along quarter circles.
---

Each constant is also in [[TweenEquations]], as `CIRC_IN`, `CIRC_OUT` and `CIRC_IN_OUT`.

Learn: [Animation](/use/learn/style-and-motion/animation/).

## CircTweenEquation()

Creates a curve of this family, for a subclass.

## IN

Starts slowly, along quarter circles.

## OUT

Ends slowly, along quarter circles.

## IN_OUT

Starts and ends slowly, along quarter circles.
