summary: Whether a node is placed inside its parent or on the canvas.
---
Learn: [Position and size](/use/learn/describing-the-ui/position-and-size/#take-a-node-out-of-its-parents-positions).

## RELATIVE

The node is placed from the top-left corner of its parent, and follows its layout and its scroll.

## ABSOLUTE

The node is placed on the canvas, out of the layout, the scroll and the content size of its parent.
