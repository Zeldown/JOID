---
summary: A side or the middle along one axis: start, center or end.
---
It sets the anchor of a node, the alignment of a layout, of a text or of a UI on the window.

Learn: [Position and size](/use/learn/describing-the-ui/position-and-size/#keep-a-point-of-a-node-in-place).

## START

The left side horizontally, the top side vertically.

## CENTER

The middle.

## END

The right side horizontally, the bottom side vertically.

## isStart()

If `true`, the value is `START`.

## isCenter()

If `true`, the value is `CENTER`.

## isEnd()

If `true`, the value is `END`.
