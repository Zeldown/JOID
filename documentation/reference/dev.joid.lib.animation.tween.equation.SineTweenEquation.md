---
summary: Easing curves along a sine.
---

Each constant is also in [[TweenEquations]], as `SINE_IN`, `SINE_OUT` and `SINE_IN_OUT`.

Learn: [Animation](/use/learn/style-and-motion/animation/).

## SineTweenEquation()

Creates a curve of this family, for a subclass.

## IN

Starts slowly, along a sine.

## OUT

Ends slowly, along a sine.

## IN_OUT

Starts and ends slowly, along a sine.
